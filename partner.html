Umoja SC is built on a model that rewrites how American youth soccer is funded. Sponsors invest, players compete at the highest level at zero cost to families, and partners get authentic community presence backed by a real tax-deductible business expense.

The Model

Umoja is funded entirely by corporate sponsors and community partners who deduct their investment as a marketing expense under IRC Section 162 — the same mechanism that funds professional jerseys, stadium naming rights, and major league advertising.

We are not a nonprofit. We are not a charity. We are partners.

Why It Works

A SPONSORSHIP, NOT A DONATION.

01
Fully Tax-Deductible

Sponsorships are ordinary and necessary business expenses under IRC §162 — structurally cleaner than charitable giving and not subject to the 2025 OBBBA limits on Section 170 deductions.

02
Authentic Visibility

Your brand on real kits, real fields, real social. Tournament visibility, community presence, and direct integration into the most-scouted youth soccer environment in the Southeast.

03
Measurable Impact

Custom impact reporting tied to your investment — player milestones, college signings, community engagement, and the brand exposure you helped make possible.
