What You Just Joined

Umoja Sports Coalition LLC is a fully sponsor-funded elite soccer club based in Atlanta, Georgia. You are one of a select group of players carrying our crest into competition. That means something.

Talent doesn't have a tax bracket. You're proof.

Everything Umoja does — your kits, your training, your travel, your college recruiting support — is covered by partners who believe in investing in your future. Your job is to honor that investment with how you train, how you compete, and how you carry yourself.

The Two Tiers

  • Open Practices — free, open training for any player ages 6–19. Anyone can come.
  • Tournament Teams — selected rosters that compete in showcases and travel tournaments. You are here.
02

Standards & Expectations

These aren't suggestions. They're how we operate.

Attendance

  • Practices are mandatory. Absences require advance notice to your head coach.
  • Tournaments are non-negotiable commitments. If you can't make a tournament weekend, communicate it weeks in advance.
  • Vacations, school events, and conflicts happen. Honest communication is what we expect.

Punctuality

  • On the field, dressed, with a ball at your feet, at the listed start time — not walking through the gate then.
  • For tournaments, arrive 90 minutes before kickoff unless told otherwise.

Kit Standards

  • Full training kit at every training session. Bring all kit pieces, every time.
  • Full match kit on game day. Sock-perfect, shin guards in, jersey tucked unless told otherwise.
  • Take care of your kit. It represents the club and the partners who made it possible.

Off-Field Conduct

How you act in public is part of the job.
  • In Umoja gear, you represent the club. Conduct yourself accordingly.
  • Social media: think before you post. College coaches are watching.
  • Schoolwork comes first. Grades affect eligibility, scholarship offers, and your future.
  • Treat teammates, opponents, refs, parents, and venue staff with the same respect.
03

Training Culture

How We Play

Umoja plays possession-based, high-tempo soccer:

  • Relentless in the press — we win the ball back as a unit
  • Disciplined in recovery — if we lose it, we recover shape immediately
  • Technically demanding — first touch out of pressure, body position before the ball arrives
  • Faster in thought, execution, and recovery — the standard at every session

What We Expect Daily

  1. Bring your best. Tired, sore, distracted — we all have those days. Bring what you have.
  2. Compete with your teammate, never against. Practice intensity makes everyone better.
  3. Receive coaching well. Eye contact, nod, apply. Don't argue at the moment of correction.
  4. Lift teammates up. Loud encouragement. Real accountability.
  5. Take care of your body. Sleep, nutrition, hydration. Your body is your tool.
04

Tournament Season

Schedule Structure

Tournament teams compete in elite Southeastern showcases throughout the season. The U19 Boys season culminates at the Jefferson Cup in Virginia — one of the most scouted youth soccer tournaments in the country.

Travel Logistics

  • Travel and lodging are covered by the club for tournament weekends.
  • Charter bus is the default mode for nearby tournaments; flights for select events.
  • You'll receive a full itinerary 7–10 days before each tournament.
  • Hotel rooms are assigned by the staff. Curfew is enforced.

What to Pack for Tournaments

  • Full match kit (jersey, shorts, socks, shin guards, cleats)
  • Training kit (pre-game warm-up plus a backup for between games)
  • Outerwear (rain jacket, warm layer — weather changes)
  • Recovery gear (foam roller, sandals, change of clothes)
  • Water bottle, snacks (high-quality, real food), phone charger
  • Schoolwork. Travel weekends still count.
05

Kit & Gear

As a tournament player, you'll receive a full kit package at zero cost:

  • Match kit — home and away jerseys, shorts, socks
  • Training kit — practice tops, shorts, training socks
  • Outerwear — team jacket and travel layer
  • Bag — team-branded travel/training bag

Sizing

You provided sizing when you applied. If anything doesn't fit when you receive your kit, tell your coach immediately — we'll work with our kit partner to get it right. Don't wear ill-fitting gear and don't trade pieces with teammates.

Care

  • Wash kit in cold water, hang dry where possible — prevents fading and shrinkage.
  • Number and name placements are heat-pressed; do not iron directly over them.
  • Lost or damaged kit pieces — tell your coach. Don't show up in the wrong gear.
06

College Recruiting

Recruiting is a process, not an event. We work it together.

Every Umoja tournament player receives individualized college recruiting support. Here's how it works:

What We Do

  • Build your player profile in our portal — stats, video, position, academic info, target levels
  • Identify schools that fit you athletically, academically, and personally
  • Make direct introductions to college coaches at appropriate moments
  • Help you produce high-quality highlight reels through our media team
  • Advocate for you when coaches call asking about character, fit, and work rate

What You Do

  1. Take your schoolwork seriously — GPA and test scores matter at every level
  2. Respond to coach communications promptly and professionally
  3. Be honest about where you're at — your level, your wants, your concerns
  4. Show up to showcases ready to perform — every game might be the one
  5. Trust the process — the right fit takes time. Don't chase the first offer if it isn't right

Realistic Levels

Division I, II, III, NAIA, and JUCO are all legitimate pathways. Your staff will help you target where you realistically project. The goal is the right fit — not the loudest logo on the jersey.

Player Portal

Every tournament player has a portal profile at portal.umojasoka.com. You'll receive login info from the registrar. The portal includes:

  • Your player bio, photo, and position
  • Performance notes from your coaches
  • Highlight reels and game film
  • College recruiting flags and target school list
  • Schedule, kit assignment, and team communication archive

Code of Conduct

By accepting your roster spot, you agree to these standards:

  1. Treat every teammate, opponent, official, parent, and venue staff member with respect.
  2. Show up on time, in the right gear, ready to work.
  3. Receive coaching well. Apply it. Ask questions in the right moments, not in the heat of the action.
  4. Take care of your body, your schoolwork, and your reputation.
  5. Represent the crest with how you train, how you compete, and how you live.
