Umoja SC is an elite youth soccer club rooted in Clarkston, Georgia. We are funded entirely by partners who see what we see — that the next generation of American soccer talent is already here, already playing, already waiting to be seen.

Where We Are

Umoja understands that building from within and creating a foundation in our community brings access and stability — a stability that has always been necessary, and always overlooked. Access that has always been longed for but never sought after.

Umoja is changing that, because Umoja belongs to us. Umoja belongs to Clarkston.

And in Clarkston, soccer is home.

Why Umoja

A CLUB UNLIKE ANY OTHER.

01
Rooted In Clarkston

Our club is structured around our coaches and players, who are all members of the community. Our staff has experienced firsthand how large the gap between access and Clarkston is. Umoja was founded to bridge the gap, allowing our players to compete at the level they are ready for, not the one they are relegated to.

02
More Than Tuition

Most clubs only offer traditional scholarships covering tuition, and in some cases travel expenses. At Umoja, being fully-funded is more than a sentiment. Access is our identity. A 1040 should never be a limitation — not on scholarship funds, not on the field.

03
Escape From Tradition

One of the biggest reasons traditional clubs charge families so much is the fees leagues charge for entry. We take an untraditional approach by providing our elite teams with a tournament structure, allowing for college coaches to see our players without passing the cost off to our families.
