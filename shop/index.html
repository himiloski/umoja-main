The collection

Made for the terraces. Worn everywhere else.

Heritage takes its cues from the shirts and jackets that have always followed the game off the pitch: collegiate lettering, old club stripes and the quiet confidence of a crest worn close to the heart.

Every piece carries the official Umoja crest and is made to order, so nothing sits in a warehouse waiting for you.
