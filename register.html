Liability Waiver

Soccer Tryout Liability Waiver, Assumption of Risk, and Parent/Guardian Consent
Umoja Sports Coalition LLC

Please read this form carefully before submitting the tryout registration.

This form applies to participation in soccer tryouts, evaluations, warmups, drills, scrimmages, and related in-person soccer activities organized by Umoja Sports Coalition LLC.

If the participant is under 18, a parent or legal guardian must read and agree to this form.

Participant Information
By submitting this registration, I confirm that:

  • The participant is at least 13 years old.
  • The participant is registering for a soccer tryout or related event.
  • All information provided in the registration form is true and complete.
  • If the participant is under 18, the person completing this consent is the participant's parent or legal guardian.

Plain-Language Summary
Soccer tryouts involve real physical risks. A player may get hurt.

By agreeing to this form:

  • The participant is choosing to take part in soccer activities.
  • The participant and parent/guardian understand that injuries can happen.
  • The participant and parent/guardian accept the normal risks of soccer tryouts.
  • The participant and parent/guardian agree not to sue Umoja Sports Coalition LLC for ordinary risks and ordinary negligence connected with the tryout, to the fullest extent allowed by Georgia and federal law.
  • This form does not waive rights that cannot legally be waived.

Assumption of Risk
I understand that soccer tryouts and related activities may be physically demanding. These activities may include running, sprinting, jumping, cutting, turning, kicking, contact with other players, contact with the ball, contact with the ground, and other athletic movements.

I understand that risks include, but are not limited to:

  • Sprains, strains, bruises, cuts, and abrasions;
  • Muscle, tendon, ligament, or joint injuries;
  • Broken bones;
  • Head, neck, back, eye, dental, or facial injuries;
  • Concussions or other head injuries;
  • Heat illness, dehydration, exhaustion, or fainting;
  • Worsening of an existing medical condition;
  • Illness or infection;
  • Serious injury, permanent disability, or death.

I understand that these risks may be caused by many things, including:

  • The participant's own actions or physical condition;
  • Other players;
  • Coaches, staff, volunteers, or spectators;
  • Field conditions;
  • Weather;
  • Equipment;
  • Emergency response time;
  • Ordinary negligence by Umoja Sports Coalition LLC or others.

I voluntarily accept these risks on behalf of myself and/or my minor child.

Health and Fitness Confirmation
I confirm that the participant is physically able to take part in soccer tryouts.

I understand that Umoja Sports Coalition LLC does not provide medical screening and is not responsible for determining whether the participant is healthy enough to participate.

I agree that the participant should not participate if the participant:

  • Is injured;
  • Feels sick;
  • Has been told by a medical provider not to participate;
  • Has symptoms that make participation unsafe;
  • Has any medical condition that could make soccer activity dangerous without medical clearance.

Medical Treatment
If the participant is injured or becomes ill during the tryout, I authorize Umoja Sports Coalition LLC and its staff, coaches, representatives, or volunteers to seek emergency medical help if they believe it is needed.

I understand that Umoja Sports Coalition LLC does not guarantee that medical personnel will be present at the tryout.

I agree that I am responsible for any medical costs related to the participant's injury, illness, or treatment.

Release of Liability
To the fullest extent allowed by Georgia and federal law, I release and agree not to sue Umoja Sports Coalition LLC, including its owners, members, managers, directors, officers, employees, contractors, coaches, trainers, volunteers, representatives, agents, sponsors, partners, successors, and assigns, for claims arising out of or related to the participant's participation in the soccer tryout or related activities.

This release includes claims for injury, illness, disability, death, property damage, or other loss, including claims based on ordinary negligence.

This release does not apply to claims that cannot legally be released, including claims based on gross negligence, willful misconduct, intentional misconduct, or other rights that may not be waived under applicable law.

Parent/Guardian Agreement for Minors
If the participant is under 18, I confirm that I am the participant's parent or legal guardian.

I give permission for the participant to take part in the soccer tryout and related activities.

I agree to the assumption of risk, release of liability, and other terms in this form on behalf of myself and the participant, to the fullest extent allowed by law.

I also agree that if I or the participant makes a claim that is released by this form, I will be responsible for the costs and expenses caused by that claim, including reasonable attorneys' fees, to the fullest extent allowed by law.

No Guarantee of Selection
I understand that participation in a tryout does not guarantee selection for any team, program, roster, scholarship, opportunity, benefit, or future event.

All selection decisions are made by Umoja Sports Coalition LLC in its discretion.

Rules and Conduct
The participant agrees to follow all tryout rules and instructions from coaches, staff, and event representatives.

Umoja Sports Coalition LLC may remove a participant from the tryout if the participant acts unsafely, violates rules, behaves disrespectfully, or creates a risk to others.

Governing Law
This form is governed by the laws of the State of Georgia, without regard to conflict-of-law rules.

Any dispute related to this form or the participant's participation in the tryout will be handled in the state or federal courts located in Georgia, unless applicable law requires a different location.

Severability
If any part of this form is found to be invalid or unenforceable, the rest of the form will still remain in effect to the fullest extent allowed by law.
