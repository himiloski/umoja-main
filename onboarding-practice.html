What Open Practice Is

Umoja's open practices are exactly that: open. If you're between 6 and 19 years old — boy or girl, club or no club — you're welcome on our field. There's no application, no fee, and no tryout to attend a session.

Show up with your boots and water. We handle the rest.

You'll train with Umoja's full coaching staff using the same possession-based methodology our tournament rosters use. Real coaching. Real development. No catch.

Who's There

  • Anyone ages 6–19 — split into age-appropriate groups when needed
  • Any gender — boys and girls train together at younger ages, with thoughtful grouping as needed
  • Any club affiliation — currently on another team? Welcome. Not on a team? Welcome.
  • Any experience level — we coach you where you are, not where someone thinks you should be
02

What to Bring

Show up with:

  • Cleats and shin guards
  • Athletic clothes appropriate for the weather
  • Water bottle (full, big)
  • A ball if you have one (we'll have extras if you don't)
  • A willingness to work, listen, and learn

What You Don't Need

  • Money — practices are free, period
  • A specific kit — wear what you have
  • An application or registration — just come
  • Prior club experience — we coach beginners and experienced players in the same environment
03

What to Expect

How a Session Runs

  1. Arrival warm-up — light technical work, free play with a ball
  2. Group splits — we may split by age, ability, or position depending on who's there
  3. Theme of the day — every session has a clear focus communicated to players
  4. Small-sided games — where real development happens
  5. Closing huddle — takeaway, recognition, what's next

How We Expect You to Behave

  • Listen when the coach is talking. Eye contact. Respect.
  • Compete hard, play fair. Win the ball, don't kick the player.
  • Encourage teammates. We build people up here.
  • Respect the field, the equipment, and each other. Pick up your trash. Help collect cones.
  • Have fun. Soccer is supposed to feel like joy. Keep that.
04

Moving Up to a Tournament Team

Many players come to open practice just to train and improve — and that's enough. But for players who want to compete for a spot on one of our tournament rosters, here's how it works:

Tournament Teams We Run

  • U19 Boys — launching Fall 2026 with 24 roster spots
  • Girls Team — age group being determined based on player interest

How to Get Considered

  1. Show up consistently. Coaches watch every session. Effort over time gets noticed.
  2. Be coachable. Apply feedback. Ask thoughtful questions. Stay open.
  3. Compete with intensity at appropriate moments. Practice intensity in real game scenarios.
  4. Show character. How you treat teammates, refs, and the field matters as much as your touch.
  5. If invited, attend tryouts. Strong open-practice players will be invited to dedicated tournament tryouts.

What Tournament Players Receive

If you make a tournament roster, your investment in showing up pays off:

  • Full custom match kit, training gear, and team outerwear
  • All tournament travel and lodging covered
  • Individualized college recruiting support
  • Access to Umoja's full coaching staff and player portal
  • All at zero cost to your family.

If You're Not Interested in the Tournament Path

Totally fine. Many of our practice players just want quality coaching in a serious environment, and that's exactly what open practices are. Come, train, get better. No pressure.
