PRIVACY POLICY

Effective Date: May 12, 2026 Last Updated: May 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Umoja Sports Coalition LLC (“Umoja SC”, “we”, “us” or “our”) collects, uses, and protects information submitted through our websites and digital properties, including umojasoka.com, umojasoka.com/apply, and portal.umojasoka.com (collectively, the “Sites”).

By submitting information through any of our Sites, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

01 Information We Collect

We collect information in two contexts: (a) general site inquiries and sponsor outreach, and (b) player interest and roster participation.

a. Sponsor & General Inquiries (umojasoka.com)

When you submit a partner deck request, schedule a call, or contact us through our main website, we may collect:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your phone number (if provided)
  • Your business or organization name (if provided)
  • Any message or inquiry you submit

b. Player Interest Forms (umojasoka.com/apply and portal.umojasoka.com)

When a player submits an interest form, we collect:

  • First and last name
  • Date of birth
  • Graduation year
  • Primary and secondary position
  • Current or most recent club and school
  • Email address and phone number
  • Highlight reel or video link (if provided)
  • Any additional information voluntarily submitted

Submission of an interest form does not constitute roster placement, a commitment by Umoja SC, or any ongoing relationship. Information collected through interest forms is used solely to notify submitters of tryout dates and details.

c. Player Portal (portal.umojasoka.com) — Rostered Players Only

Access to the Player Portal is restricted to players who have been formally added to the Umoja SC roster. No player will be added to the roster or granted portal access without their knowledge and explicit consent. Within the portal, we may collect and store:

  • Athletic profile information (name, graduation year, position, height, dominant foot, phone number, previous clubs, jersey number)
  • Film and highlight reel submissions and requests
  • Recruiting course progress and modules completion
  • College tracking data (internal use only — not shared with third parties)
  • Schedule and session information
  • Communications from coaching staff

02 How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

ContextHow We Use It
Sponsor & general inquiries To respond to your inquiry, send requested materials, and communicate about partnership opportunities
Player interest forms To notify you of tryout dates, times, and logistics
Player portal To manage player development, recruiting preparation, film, scheduling, and internal communications

We do not use any information collected through our Sites for advertising, profiling, or sale to third parties.

03 Information We Do Not Share

We do not sell, rent, license, or otherwise disclose your personal information to third parties for their own purposes. Specifically:

  • Player portal data, including college tracking information, athletic profiles, and film, is for internal use only by Umoja SC coaching and administrative staff.
  • Interest form submissions are not shared with any outside party.
  • Sponsor inquiry data is not shared with any outside party.

04 Third-Party Services

Our Sites use third-party tools to facilitate hosting, scheduling, and form submissions. These may include Squarespace, Netlify, Airtable, and Formspree. Each of these services processes data under its own privacy policies. We encourage you to review those policies directly. We do not grant these providers the right to use your data for any purposes beyond facilitating our services.

05 Minors’ Privacy

Umoja SC works with youth athletes, some of whom may be under the age of 18 or under the age of 13.

Players under 13: We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13 without verifiable parental consent. If you are under 13, please do not submit an interest form or any personal information through our Sites without a parent or guardian’s involvement. If we become aware that we have collected personal information from a child under 13 without parental consent, we will delete that information promptly.

Players age 13–17: We encourage parents and guardians to review this Privacy Policy with their child before submitting any information through our Sites. By submitting an interest form on behalf of or together with a minor, a parent or guardian acknowledges this Privacy Policy.

Roster players who are minors: Players added to the Umoja SC roster who are under 18 will have their portal access and data collection acknowledged as part of the roster onboarding process, which will include parental or guardian consent where required.

If you believe a minor has submitted information without appropriate parental consent, please contact us immediately at the address below, and we will delete the information.

06 Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purpose for which it was collected:

  • Interest form submissions are retained through the conclusion of the tryout and roster selection process for the applicable season, after which they are deleted unless the individual is added to the roster.
  • Sponsor inquiry data is retained for the duration of any active or prospective partnership relationship.
  • Player portal data is retained for the duration of a player’s participation in the program and for a reasonable period thereafter for administrative purposes.

You may request deletion of your information at any time by contacting us at the address below.

07 Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information you submit through our Sites. However, no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security and encourage you to avoid submitting sensitive information (such as government ID numbers or financial information) through our Sites.

08 Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request the deletion of your information
  • Withdraw consent to our use of your information at any time

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the information below. We will respond to all requests within a reasonable timeframe and in the order in which they are received.

09 Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or applicable law. When we do, we will update the “Last Updated” date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically. Continued use of our Sites following any update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
