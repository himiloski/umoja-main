The Club
Umoja SC is an elite boys program in Clarkston, Georgia built on one conviction: talent doesn't have a tax bracket.

We play possession-based, high-tempo soccer — relentless in the press, disciplined in recovery, and technically demanding in every session. We develop players who think faster, run harder, and compete more.

On and off the pitch, we're family.

THE PROGRAM

Elite training, competitive tournaments, college recruiting support, and complete player development — every cost covered, every player invested in.

01

Technical Training

Possession-based sessions built around tactical intelligence, technical precision, and game-speed decision making. Position-specific development — including dedicated goalkeeper training — is part of the structure.

02

Physical Development

Strength, conditioning, and endurance programming designed to build athletes who can sustain intensity across a full match and a full tournament weekend.

03

Competition

The coaching staff hand selects elite local showcases for our players to attend. We will end our season at the Jefferson Cup in Virginia — one of the most prestigious tournaments in American youth soccer.

04

Player Development

We holistically develop our players. Umoja firmly upholds the belief that our team are people first. We focus on building strong-willed, confident, well-rounded members of our community who just so happen to play soccer.

YOUR NEXT
CHAPTER

Every Umoja player receives individualized college recruiting support. We work diligently with each athlete to identify the right fit and actively connect them with college coaches.

Our players will never be left wondering if they have done enough. Umoja's staff builds comprehensive player profiles through our player portal, making the recruiting process easier for all involved.

DAY ONE

When you or your player makes the Umoja roster, you show up, and everything is waiting. Players receive custom kits, branded gear, team outerwear — no fees, no fundraisers, no hidden costs.

Every player is announced on our social channel and given a presence on our player portal. We treat players just like family from the very start.
