Delivery & returns

How orders from the Umoja Club Shop are made, shipped and looked after.

How your order is made

Every piece is made to order by our production partner once you check out. Nothing is made until you order, which keeps waste down.

You’ll get an order confirmation by email straight away, and tracking details when your order ships.

Shipping

Shipping options and costs are shown at checkout before you pay.

Returns & exchanges

If something arrives damaged or wrong, email info@umojasoka.com with your order number and a photo, and we’ll make it right.

Sizing

Each product page has a size guide with garment measurements from the maker. If you’re between sizes, email us and we’ll help.

Payments & security

Checkout, payment and receipts are handled by Shopify. Umoja never sees or stores your card details.

The Umoja Circle

Shop orders and Circle membership are separate. Buying from the shop doesn’t make you a Circle member, and Circle member rewards aren’t sold here. Members never pay for a reward they’ve earned. Learn about the Circle.
