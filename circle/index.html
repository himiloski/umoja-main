The UmojaCircle

One club. One community. A shared future.

Umoja is a free-to-play football academy in Clarkston, Georgia, opening its doors in August 2027. The Circle is its supporter community: people everywhere who love the game and want to see what it can become. Join, take your number, and help build the club from day one.

    Membership terms

    Good to know

    The short version of how membership works. Questions? Email fundraising@umojasoka.com.

    Proposed terms for Juliana's review

    Who can join?

    Anyone, anywhere, who believes in Umoja. You do not need a player in the club or a connection to Clarkston. Physical rewards ship within the United States at launch. Members outside the U.S. receive the full digital membership, and we will share international shipping options as they become available.

    Can I cancel or change my level?

    Yes, anytime. Cancel and your membership benefits continue through the period you have already paid for. Move up a level and your new benefits begin with your next payment, with your completed payments still counting toward rewards. Move down and the change takes effect on your next billing date.

    What if a payment does not go through?

    We will send you a note so you can update your card. Your membership stays active for 14 days while that happens. Reward progress picks up where it left off once payments resume.

    When do physical rewards arrive?

    Monthly members receive their welcome package after three completed monthly payments and their level's annual merchandise package after six. Annual members qualify right away, with delivery in 30 to 60 days depending on production. Commemorative gifts come once per membership year, and a new annual package is earned each membership year.

    Can I choose digital only?

    Absolutely. Tick "digital only" at checkout or let us know anytime. You keep every digital benefit, every event invitation and your founding status.

    Is my membership tax-deductible?

    Umoja Soccer Club, Inc. is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit. Your membership is deductible to the extent allowed by law, which means the amount you give minus the fair market value of any goods or services you receive. Your receipt will show that value. Members who choose digital only receive a receipt that reflects it.

    What does Founding Member status include?

    Everyone who joins before August 1, 2027 is a Founding Member for life, with a founding number assigned in order of joining. The designation stays with you permanently, even if your paid membership ends. Paid membership benefits continue while your membership is active.

    How do gift memberships work?

    Gift memberships are annual. You choose the level and we send a digital gift certificate on the date you pick. The recipient activates their membership with their own shipping details, and gift memberships made before August 1, 2027 carry founding status.

    What can members vote on?

    Members help choose things like merchandise designs. These votes are just for fun. They do not create voting rights in the nonprofit corporation, governance rights or any ownership interest.

    Refunds?

    If something is not right, email us within 30 days of a payment and we will refund it, as long as that payment's physical rewards have not shipped yet.

    How is my information used?

    Payments are handled securely by Givebutter. Umoja never sees or stores your card details. We never sell or share member information, and your name only appears in public with your permission. We do not identify individual players or scholarship recipients in member content.
