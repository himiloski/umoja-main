01
Phase 01 · The House

A place
that’s theirs.

Umoja was created to be more than just a club; we are a safe space for our players to learn and become the people they are meant to be. A house is merely a physical embodiment of our core mission. Facilities provide our players wih the greatest gift of all: consistency.

KitchenWell-balanced meals, cooking lessons, and meal prep options.
Training FieldPickup games, intra club scrimmages, and trainings conducted by Umoja staff.
Tennis/Basketball/Futsal Courtcross-training strengthening technical abilities.
Private PoolWater safety lessons, endurance training, and recovery.
Creative Spacea space to promote self-expression off the pitch.

Move 02 · The Teams

Room for
everyone.

Today we serve 45 boys across U12 and U18/19 age groups. Next we will extend our programs to girls, and younger boys reaching six teams by the 2028-2029 season.
