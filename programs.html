What We Offer

TWO WAYS
TO PLAY.

Open practices for anyone who wants to train. Tournament teams for players ready to compete at the highest level. Every program covered, every player invested in.

The Model
Umoja SC opens its training environment to any player ages 6–19, regardless of gender, club affiliation, or background. From that environment, we build the tournament teams that represent our community on the biggest stages.

Practices are open. Roster spots are earned.
Open To All

OPEN
PRACTICES

Ages 6–19  ·  Any Gender  ·  Free

Our open practices are exactly that — open. If you're between 6 and 19, you're welcome on the field. No tryouts to attend, no fees to pay, no club affiliation required.

You'll train alongside our tournament rosters and learn from the same coaching staff that prepares our players for Jefferson Cup. It's the entry point to everything Umoja does.

For some players, it's the destination. For others, it's the start of the path to one of our tournament rosters.

Competitive Rosters

TOURNAMENT
TEAMS

U19 Boys  ·  Girls Team  ·  By Roster

Two competitive rosters represent Umoja SC at premier tournaments across the Southeast. Players are selected from our open practice pool and through dedicated tryouts.

HOW IT WORKS

Open practices are designed to meet players where they are. Bring your boots and water. Everything else, we handle.

01

Show Up

Practices are open. No registration form required to attend your first session. Just bring your gear and a willingness to work.

02

Train With Us

Sessions are run by Umoja's full coaching staff. Possession-based methodology, age-appropriate programming, and serious development.

03

Get Noticed

Coaches watch every session. Strong performers are invited to tryouts for our competitive rosters — boys and girls.

04

Earn The Crest

Players selected for a tournament roster receive full kits, travel, and recruiting support. Same standard for every player.
