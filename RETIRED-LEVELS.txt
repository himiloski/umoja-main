## programs.html — the 'Play the game' section (Foundations + Rec)

<section class="s-sec"><div class="wrap">
  <p class="s-lbl">Play the game &mdash; complete programs, not a waiting room</p>

  <div class="pg" id="foundations">
    <div class="pg-head"><h3>Foundations</h3><span class="tag">Forming</span></div>
    <span class="id">Where a child learns to play.</span>
    <p>One coached practice and one Saturday play day each week &mdash; a teaching day and a playing day. Small-sided, built entirely around touches on the ball, run by the coaches in house with no referees and no league table. At this age the coach decides more about a player&rsquo;s future than the fixture does, which is why Umoja staffs this level deliberately rather than with whoever is available.</p>
    <p>Nobody is assessed and nobody is cut. A child who has never kicked a ball and a child who already plays every day are both in the right place here.</p>
    <div class="pg-facts">
      <div><span class="k">Ages</span><span class="v">U6 &ndash; U10</span></div>
      <div><span class="k">Training</span><span class="v">One 60-min practice a week</span></div>
      <div><span class="k">Season</span><span class="v">13-week periods</span></div>
      <div><span class="k">Travel</span><span class="v">None</span></div>
    </div>
    <p class="fits"><b>This is right for you if</b> your child is new to the game, or young enough that the only thing that matters is time on the ball.</p>
  </div>

  <div class="pg" id="rec">
    <div class="pg-head"><h3>Rec</h3><span class="tag">Forming</span></div>
    <span class="id">Real football, on your own terms.</span>
    <p>A proper season in the RIAS recreational league, without the commitment or the calendar of the competitive side. Two practices a week with volunteer coaches, one local event in the year, a team, a coach who knows your name &mdash; and the rest of your week back.</p>
    <p>Choosing Rec is a decision about time, not ability. Plenty of players here could play competitively and have decided they want a life outside sport as well. Nobody in Rec is being evaluated. That said, our coaches are asked to speak up if a player is clearly playing below their level, because sometimes a family simply did not know the competitive program existed or assumed it was out of reach financially.</p>
    <div class="pg-facts">
      <div><span class="k">Ages</span><span class="v">U11 &ndash; U19</span></div>
      <div><span class="k">Training</span><span class="v">Two 75-min practices a week</span></div>
      <div><span class="k">Season</span><span class="v">13-week periods, RIAS league</span></div>
      <div><span class="k">Travel</span><span class="v">None</span></div>
    </div>
    <p class="fits"><b>This is right for you if</b> your player loves the game and your family needs the week to belong to school, work, faith or family.</p>
  </div>

## programs.html — Development and Evolution blocks

<div class="pg" id="development">
    <div class="pg-head"><h3>Development</h3><span class="tag">Forming</span></div>
    <span class="id">The entry point to competitive football.</span>
    <p>The technical work continues, but players start being asked tactical questions &mdash; where to stand, when to press, what the player next to them is doing. Training moves from several touches to several decisions.</p>
    <p>This level sits inside the years where most children quit sport altogether, so it is built to keep them rather than to sort them. The demands are real and the intensity is deliberately not cranked up. A twelve-year-old has enough going on.</p>
    <div class="pg-facts">
      <div><span class="k">Ages</span><span class="v">U11 &ndash; U19</span></div>
      <div><span class="k">Training</span><span class="v">Two 75-min practices a week</span></div>
      <div><span class="k">Season</span><span class="v">Full year &middot; two local events</span></div>
      <div><span class="k">Travel</span><span class="v">Minimal</span></div>
    </div>
    <p class="fits"><b>This is right for you if</b> your player wants to be coached seriously and is ready to train more than once a week.</p>
    <p class="fits" style="border-left-color:var(--line)"><b>U15 and above:</b> 24 weeks at the normal club training load plus a supplemental training pool equivalent to 12 further weeks, built around the high-school soccer season.</p>
  </div>

  <div class="pg" id="evolution">
    <div class="pg-head"><h3>Evolution</h3><span class="tag now">Playing now</span></div>
    <span class="id">A step up in standard, and in who you play.</span>
    <p>The same work, held to a higher line, against stronger opposition across metro Atlanta. Players are expected at training rather than invited to it, and the coach is planning a season rather than a session.</p>
    <p>What changes here is the standard of the opponent as much as the standard of the work. Playing better teams asks questions a training ground cannot.</p>
    <div class="pg-facts">
      <div><span class="k">Ages</span><span class="v">U11 &ndash; U19</span></div>
      <div><span class="k">Training</span><span class="v">Three 85-min practices a week</span></div>
      <div><span class="k">Season</span><span class="v">Three local events &middot; one regional</span></div>
      <div><span class="k">Travel</span><span class="v">Some, in state</span></div>
    </div>
    <p class="fits"><b>This is right for you if</b> your player is committed to the season and wants to be tested by better opposition.</p>
    <p class="fits" style="border-left-color:var(--line)"><b>U15 and above:</b> 24 weeks at the normal club training load plus a supplemental training pool equivalent to 12 further weeks, built around the high-school soccer season.</p>
  </div>


## programs.html — original intro and 'today' copy

<h2>Two ways to play.</h2>
<p class="lede">Foundations and Rec are complete programs in their own right. The four levels listed below them are a pathway a player moves through as they develop.</p>
<p class="s-lbl">Chase the game &mdash; each level asks for more, and gives more</p>
<p>Three squads are training and playing: a U12 side at Evolution, a U12 side at Premier, and U18/19. The rest of the pathway is forming, and tryouts in early 2027 open the age groups in between.</p>
