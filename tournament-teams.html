Umoja SC fields two tournament rosters built to compete at the highest level of youth soccer in the Southeast. Same coaching staff. Same investment. Same expectation: win and develop.

  • Birth Years2007 — 2008
  • TryoutsSummer 2026
  • SeasonFall 2026 — Spring 2027
  • Marquee TournamentJefferson Cup

Umoja's inaugural tournament roster. The U19 boys team is the team that started it all — built around the founding mission of breaking down financial barriers for elite-level players in the Clarkston area.

This roster plays a possession-based, high-tempo style — relentless in the press, disciplined in recovery, technically demanding in every session. The schedule is built around premier Southeastern showcases, with the season culminating at the Jefferson Cup in Virginia — one of the most scouted tournaments in American youth soccer.

Every roster spot is earned. Every player is invested in.
