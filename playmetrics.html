Join Umoja!

Sign up to play. It is fast and free.

Sign Up

You need

  • Your phone
  • Your email
  • Your birthday
  • A grown-up’s name and phone number

Steps

  1. Tap the big Sign Up button.
  2. Make an account. Use your email. Pick a password. Write it down!
  3. Add yourself as the player. Type your name and birthday.
  4. Pick your team. Your coach will tell you which one.
  5. Type a grown-up’s name and phone number.
  6. Tap Submit. You are done!
